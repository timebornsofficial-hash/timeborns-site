Key Features

The clues that bring a day back to life

📸 Representative Photo

Choose one photo to help bring a day back. Dayfall keeps an optimized, app-managed local copy.

📍 Meaningful Places

Recall places where you stayed for a while. Place records focus on significant stays, without storing a continuous travel route.

✍️ Short Notes

Leave a small note for the moments worth remembering.

💳 Payment Traces

Add payment clues yourself, by typing or pasting text. No bank or card account connection and no automatic SMS access.

📅 Events & Anniversaries

Record birthdays, anniversaries, appointments, and other important dates — with local reminders.

🏡 Home Widgets

Calendar, Today, and Upcoming bring your days and important dates to your home screen.

Additional Features

More ways to remember

Backup & Restore

Create a backup file encrypted with a password you choose, then choose where to save it. Use your file and password to restore your memories.

Memory Card

Turn a day's memories into a card to save or share. If you choose to create a card, a rewarded ad is requested after your confirmation. The card opens after the ad reward is confirmed.

App Permissions

What we ask for, and why

Choose which permissions to allow for photos, place memories, and local reminders.

📸 Photos

For selecting a representative photo and finding photo-based memory cues. Library access is requested on first use of the photo feature. Where supported, manual selection through the system photo picker remains available with selected-photo access or after declining library access.

📍 Location, including background location

With your permission, Dayfall uses location in the background to remember meaningful places where you stayed, even when the app is closed or not in use. It records significant stays of roughly 15 minutes or more, without storing a continuous travel route.

Android's geocoding service helps turn coordinates into approximate place names and may use network services. Location accuracy and availability vary by device and system settings.

🔔 Notifications

For local reminders about birthdays, anniversaries, events you add, and gentle prompts to look back on your day. These reminders are scheduled on your device.

Battery optimization settings may affect background place detection and reminder delivery.

Honest Limitations

What Dayfall does not do

Dayfall is a tool to help you recall memories — not a system that records everything perfectly.

  • Location accuracy varies depending on device conditions, OS policy, battery optimization, and GPS environment.
  • Place names may identify a neighborhood or district rather than a precise venue.
  • Place detection and local reminders may be delayed or missed because of permissions, battery restrictions, or system settings.
  • Keep your encrypted backup file and password safe. Dayfall excludes locally stored memory records from Android automatic cloud backup. Device-to-device transfer may be available on supported Android versions and devices, depending on system settings and the transfer method; it does not replace your manual backup.
  • Complete recording, backup, and recovery are not guaranteed.

Your choices matter: Saving or sharing a Memory Card and choosing a backup destination can place copies outside Dayfall. Review the Privacy Policy for Android backup rules, device transfer, and third-party service processing.
