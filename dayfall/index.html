Dayfall

A memory calendar for the days you lived.

"그날 뭐 했지?"

Dayfall is not a traditional schedule planner. It is not about what you are going to do. It is a quiet memory calendar that helps you remember the days you have already lived.

With photos, places, short notes, payment traces, and events, Dayfall helps you bring back the shape of a day. Your data stays on your device — no login, no server required.

"What did I actually do that day?" — Dayfall helps you remember.

Key Features

The clues that bring a day back to life

📸 Representative Photo

Set a photo that best captures the atmosphere of the day. Choose from your gallery or take a new one with the camera.

📍 Place History

Review the places you visited throughout the day in chronological order. Records focus on locations where you stayed for a meaningful amount of time.

✍️ One-Line Memo

Summarize the day in a single sentence — a space to write how you want to remember this day.

💳 Payment Traces

A supporting clue to recall where you were and what you did. Based on manual records — no automatic financial connection.

📅 Events & Anniversaries

Record birthdays, anniversaries, appointments, and other important dates — with local reminders.

🔒 Local Storage

All data stays on your device. No login required. Nothing is sent to the cloud.

App Permissions

What we ask for, and why

Dayfall requests only the minimum permissions needed for its core features.

📸 Photo Library Access

Used to select a representative photo for each day and to display photo metadata (such as capture date and time) as memory cues. Photos are never uploaded or transmitted outside your device. Timeborns never accesses your photos.

📍 Location (including "Always Allow")

Used to reconstruct which places you visited during the day. Only locations where you stayed for roughly 15 minutes or more are recorded — not your full movement path. Location data stays on your device and is never sent externally. Background location may be needed to capture place history when the app is not open.

🔔 Notifications

Used to send local reminders for birthdays, anniversaries, and events you have added. All notifications are generated locally on your device — no remote server involved.

Limitations

What Dayfall does not do

Dayfall is a tool to help you recall memories — not a system that records everything perfectly.

  • Location accuracy varies depending on device conditions, OS policy, battery optimization, and GPS environment.
  • Place records are based on locations where you stayed for approximately 15 minutes or more.
  • Full movement paths (continuous routes) are not stored.
  • No automatic financial connection — payment records are manual notes only.
  • Backup and restore are local only — no cloud sync is provided.
  • Data stored locally may not be recoverable if your device is lost or reset without a prior backup.
  • Dayfall is not a surveillance tool. It is a personal memory aid for your own use only.

Important: Everything recorded in Dayfall exists only on your device. Timeborns does not access, collect, or process your memory records, location data, or photos.
