Privacy Policy

Dayfall is built around local-first memory storage. This policy explains permissions, backups, sharing, and third-party services.

Effective Date: October 27, 2026  ·  App: Dayfall  ·  Developer: Timeborns (timebornsofficial@gmail.com)

1. Overview

Dayfall is a local-first memory calendar. It helps you remember past days through photos, places, notes, events, and payment traces you enter, paste, or choose to record from supported card and bank app notifications.

Dayfall does not require a TimeBorns account or provide a TimeBorns cloud sync service. Your memory records are stored locally in the app and are not uploaded to a TimeBorns server.

Android device-to-device transfer, place-name lookup, and the advertising SDK can involve separate third-party processing. You can also choose to export a backup or save or share a Memory Card. These cases are explained below.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following types of information may be stored locally on your device as a result of using Dayfall:

  • Representative photos, stored as optimized, app-managed local copies with thumbnails
  • Location records derived from places you stayed for a significant amount of time (see Section 3.2)
  • Text notes and memos you write within the app
  • Payment details entered manually, extracted from text you paste, or recorded from supported app notifications when you enable that feature; saved fields, temporary queued fields, and text retained in older records are described in Section 4
  • Calendar events, birthdays, anniversaries, and other important dates you enter
  • App preferences and settings

Dayfall does not upload these memory records to a TimeBorns server. Backup, sharing, and third-party processing are described in Sections 3, 5, and 6.

3. Permissions Used

3.1 Photos and System Photo Selection

Dayfall requests photo library access on first use of a photo feature. Full library access supports finding a representative photo for a day automatically. On supported Android versions, you can allow full access, allow selected photos, or decline library access.

Manual selection uses the system photo selection interface, including Android's Photo Picker where supported. This is separate from broader library access: manual selection remains available where supported with selected-photo access or after declining library access. Dayfall stores an optimized local copy and thumbnail of the chosen image. There is no in-app camera capture feature in Android v1.0.

Dayfall does not upload your photos to a TimeBorns server. Copies may be included in backups or in a Memory Card you choose to save or share (see Sections 5 and 6).

3.2 Location (Including Background Location)

With your permission, Dayfall uses location, including background location, to remember meaningful places where you stayed even when the app is closed or not in use. Android may label background access "Allow all the time."

How location data is used:

  • In Android v1.0.2, a stay is confirmed after roughly 10 minutes of usable location observations within an area of about 100 metres in radius: an initial five-minute observation period followed by about five more minutes. Confirmation requires a fresh location reading within the area; elapsed time alone is not enough. Continuous movement paths are not stored.
  • Clear movement outside the area can reset an unconfirmed stay. For a confirmed stay, Dayfall uses a roughly five-minute departure check, taking location uncertainty into account. An in-range reading cancels a pending departure; uncertain readings do not by themselves confirm departure.
  • Background location access, if granted, allows place detection even when the app is not actively open, so your day's location story can be reconstructed later.
  • Stay records are stored locally. Dayfall can use bundled administrative-area data for South Korea, Japan, Taiwan, and Singapore to look up approximate place names on the device. It also uses Android's system geocoding service when needed, passing coordinates to that service. The system provider may process the lookup over a network. Dayfall does not upload these coordinates to a TimeBorns server; system-provider processing is separate from TimeBorns cloud synchronization.
  • Names may describe a neighborhood or district rather than an exact venue.
  • Location accuracy and timing depend on your device hardware, GPS conditions, OS restrictions, battery optimization, delayed location readings, and network environment. Dayfall cannot guarantee complete or perfectly accurate location capture.

3.3 Notifications

Dayfall requests notification permission to send local reminders for events you have added — such as birthdays, anniversaries, significant dates, and calendar entries — and gentle prompts to record or reflect on your day.

Dayfall reminder notifications are generated locally on your device. No remote notification server is involved.

3.4 Optional Notification Access (Android)

Automatic payment recording requires Android's separate notification access, in addition to enabling the feature in Dayfall. This is different from permission for Dayfall to show its own notifications. At the operating-system level, this access can expose notifications from other apps, including apps unrelated to payments. Dayfall applies its own narrower filter: it checks the source app against its supported card and bank app list before reading notification text, then processes notifications that appear to describe a payment. Other apps' notification content is ignored. The processing, storage, and controls are described in Section 4.

4. Payment / Transaction Records

Dayfall lets you add payment details as memory cues by typing them yourself or pasting payment message text. Pasting provides text you choose to supply from the clipboard or input field; it does not read your SMS inbox. For new records in Android v1.0.2, the app stores the payment fields and a simple manual/paste marker, not the original pasted message.

Original pasted text saved by earlier versions may remain in existing records or in backups you created. Updating the app does not automatically remove that older text, and restoring an older backup can restore it. You can delete the associated records in the app; copies in exported backup files must be managed separately.

Dayfall does not connect to financial institutions, banks, card networks, or any financial API. It does not automatically access or read your SMS inbox. Payment records are stored locally and serve as personal memory cues only. Android v1.0.2 also offers the optional notification feature below.

4.1 Optional Payment Notification Auto-Record

This feature is off by default. It receives new notifications only after you enable it in Dayfall Settings and grant Android notification access. From supported card and bank apps, Dayfall analyzes eligible notification text in memory on your device. The automatic recording path does not write the original notification text to its temporary queue or payment database.

  • Parsed fields: Approval, cancellation, or decline type; amount and currency; and merchant and transaction time when present. A record can be created without a merchant name or transaction time. Notification arrival time is used for ordering and, when necessary, the record's date; it is not presented as a known transaction time.
  • Temporary storage: A private local queue holds the parsed fields, source app package, a duplicate-detection hash, and notification arrival time. It holds at most 100 entries. When a new, nonduplicate entry is added, entries whose notification arrival time is more than three days old are removed. This is not a timer-based guarantee of deletion exactly after three days. The queue is outside Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • Saved records: The app stores payment fields, a notification-source marker, approval/cancellation status, duplicate-detection information, and cancellation-link information. It attempts to suppress duplicate notifications. A cancellation is linked to an approval when a single matching record is found; otherwise, it can be kept as a separate cancelled record. Declined or failed notices are not saved as payment records.
  • Limits: Supported apps, notification wording, available fields, Android access restrictions, and delivery timing affect recognition. Dayfall does not guarantee that every payment or cancellation will be captured or matched correctly.

Dayfall does not upload notification text or these payment fields to a TimeBorns server. This feature does not connect to your bank or card account, initiate payments, or read your SMS inbox. Advertising SDK processing and Android system geocoding remain separate, as described elsewhere in this policy.

4.2 Stopping Automatic Recording and Deleting Payment Data

Turning the feature off in Dayfall Settings disables its notification listener, stops pending parser work, and clears the temporary queue. You can also revoke notification access in Android Settings to stop new notification delivery. Revoking access does not itself clear the queue: previously received fields may still be processed when Dayfall next reads it. The queue file is removed when Dayfall takes its entries for processing.

Turning the feature off or revoking access does not delete saved payment records. You can delete records in the app. Settings > Delete all data removes saved records and clears queued payment fields. To stop future notification collection, also turn the feature off or revoke notification access.

Saved payment records may be included in an encrypted manual backup you choose to create or in eligible device-to-device transfer under Section 6. Deleting records in Dayfall does not remove copies in backup files you saved elsewhere. The temporary notification queue is excluded from Android backup and transfer.

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Advertising

Dayfall's Android v1.0 build integrates Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). Banner ads appear at the bottom of the calendar home and day detail screens when an ad loads. A rewarded ad is requested only after you choose and confirm Memory Card creation; the card opens after the ad reward is confirmed.

Dayfall does not attach your memory records, notes, photos, pasted payment text, or stored place history to its ad requests. This is separate from the SDK's own data processing: Google describes collection and sharing of IP addresses, device or account identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. IP addresses may be used to estimate a device's general location. Processing depends on SDK configuration and applicable device and consent settings.

See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure and Privacy Policy. Advertising ID controls are available in Android settings; available choices vary by device.

5.2 In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are not available in Android v1.0.

5.3 Crash Reporting and Analytics

Android v1.0 does not integrate a separate app analytics or crash reporting service such as Firebase Crashlytics. The advertising SDK's own diagnostics and analytics are described in Section 5.1.

Recalling your locally stored memories does not require a TimeBorns account.

5.4 Memory Card Sharing

A Memory Card is generated on your device from the day's memories. When you choose to save or share it, a copy goes to the destination or app you select. That destination's handling of the shared content is separate from Dayfall's local storage.

6. Backup and Data Recovery

Dayfall backup files: You can manually create a backup encrypted with a password you choose, then select its storage destination through the system file interface. Restoring requires the backup file and its password. A destination you choose may be a local folder or a third-party cloud storage service. The selected provider's own terms and privacy practices apply. TimeBorns does not operate a cloud backup or remote recovery service.

Android backup and device-to-device transfer: Dayfall's backup rules exclude its locally stored memory records from Android automatic cloud backup. On Android 9 and later, these records may be transferred between devices, depending on the device, operating-system behavior, settings, and transfer method. On Android 7 through 8.1, these records are also excluded from device-to-device transfer by the app's backup rules. Device transfer is separate from the encrypted backup files you create yourself and does not replace your manual backup. It does not guarantee that all photos or database records will be transferred or restored.

Keep your own encrypted backup file and password safe. If your device is lost, reset, or replaced without a usable backup, your memories may not be recoverable. You are responsible for maintaining your own backups; successful backup and complete restoration are not guaranteed.

Deleting local data: You can delete memory records in the app. Settings > Delete all data removes local records and app-managed photo and Memory Card copies. This does not delete original photos in your device's photo library, backup files or copies you have saved outside Dayfall, or data processed separately by third-party services.

7. Children's Privacy

Dayfall is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app in a way that raises privacy concerns, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of Dayfall after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Changes that materially affect how your data is handled will be communicated through an in-app notice or an updated app store listing description.
