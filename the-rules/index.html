The Rules

Decide once. Keep the rest.

결정은 한 번. 나머지는 지키는 일.

Create your own rules and keep the promises you make to yourself.

The Rules is not a to-do list. Tasks come from the outside; rules come from you. You decide once what kind of person you intend to be, write it down as a rule, and then the only question left each day is whether you kept it.

Check off a rule and your streak grows. Miss it and the streak starts over. Nothing else to manage — your rules live on your own device, with no account to create.

Key Features

Your standards, kept simple

Create your own rules

Write the rules you want to live by, in your own words. No templates, no assigned goals — only what you decided for yourself.

Daily or Monthly

Every rule runs on one of two rhythms. Some things belong to each day; some belong to each month. Nothing more complicated than that.

Track success and streaks

One tap marks a rule kept. Your streak grows while you keep it, and honestly resets when you do not.

Keep your own standards

No feed, no comparison, no social pressure. The only standard being measured is the one you set.
