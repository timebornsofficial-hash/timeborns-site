Privacy Policy

The rules you write and the records you keep stay on your own device. This policy explains what that means in practice, and what happens where it does not apply.

Effective Date: September 28, 2026  ·  App: The Rules  ·  Developer: Timeborns (timebornsofficial@gmail.com)

1. Overview

The Rules is a personal rulebook app. You write your own rules, mark them as kept, and build streaks over time. It is available as a mini app inside Toss and as a standalone Android app distributed through Google Play.

Your rule text, success records, streaks, points, achievements, and app settings are not sent to a Timeborns server. The Rules has no Timeborns account system, no Timeborns cloud-sync service, and no backend that receives your rule content.

Platform services, advertising providers, and Android system services may process limited technical data separately from your rule content. Those cases are explained below.

2. What is Stored on Your Device

The following is created by you and stored locally by the app:

  • The text of the rules you write
  • Whether each rule is Daily or Monthly
  • Your success records and the dates attached to them
  • Your current and past streak values
  • Your in-app points balance and how many rule slots you have unlocked
  • Basic app settings

Timeborns has no ability to read any of this. It is not transmitted to us, and we hold no copy of it.

3. What the Core App Does Not Request or Store

  • No Timeborns account credentials
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or calendar access
  • No location data
  • No health or biometric data
  • No payment or financial information

The Android app requests internet and network access for advertising and may request notification permission for local reminders. These permissions do not give Timeborns access to your rule text or success records.

In-app points used to unlock rule slots are an internal app value only. They are not Toss Points, not currency, not redeemable, and have no cash value. No payment processing takes place inside The Rules.

4. Advertising

The Rules is supported by advertising. The advertising provider differs by platform: the Toss version uses advertising components available through the Toss mini app platform, while the Android version uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

Advertising providers may process technical, device, delivery, and ad-interaction information under their own privacy policies in order to provide and measure advertising. Timeborns does not send your rule text, success records, streaks, or achievement data to an advertising provider for ad delivery.

Where the app offers a rewarded ad, watching it is voluntary. Choosing not to watch it does not remove access to rules or records you already have.

5. Platform and System Services

5.1 Toss

When you use The Rules inside Toss, your use also takes place in an environment operated by Toss. Toss's own privacy policy and terms apply to that environment. Timeborns does not receive your Toss account credentials or a copy of your rules.

5.2 Android and Google Play

The standalone Android app is distributed through Google Play and uses Android system services. Local reminders are generated on the device after notification permission is granted.

The Android app allows eligible app data to participate in Android's standard backup and device-transfer mechanisms. Depending on your device and Google account settings, Android may preserve eligible app data for backup or transfer it to another device. This is an operating-system service, not a Timeborns cloud-sync service, and Timeborns does not operate or control those backups.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your active rules and records remain in the app until you delete them or clear the app's local data. Timeborns does not maintain a server-side copy of your rule content and cannot restore it from a Timeborns server.

On Android, an operating-system backup or device-transfer copy may exist separately according to your Android and Google backup settings. Its retention and deletion are controlled through those platform settings, not by Timeborns.

7. Children

The Rules is not directed at children. Timeborns does not knowingly operate an account database containing children's rule content or personal profiles. Platform and advertising providers process data, if any, under their own policies and legal obligations.

8. Your Rights

Timeborns does not maintain a server-side account, profile, or copy of your rule content. You can view, edit, and delete your rules and records directly within the app.

Data handled independently by Toss, Google Play, Google Mobile Ads, or Android system backup is governed by the controls and policies of those services. If you believe Timeborns itself holds information about you and would like it addressed, contact us below.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as The Rules, its distribution platforms, or its service providers change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
